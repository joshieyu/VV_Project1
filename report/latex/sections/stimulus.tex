\section{Constrained-random stimulus}
\label{sec:crv}



A \texttt{pyvsc} transaction randomizes \texttt{wr\_en}, \texttt{rd\_en},
and 8-bit \texttt{wr\_data}; reference occupancy and fill/drain phase are
nonrandom inputs. All four request pairs are legal, including blocked writes
at full and reads at empty. Uniform mode gives each enable a nominal 50\%
rate. Tuned mode uses 75\% write/25\% read in the interior fill phase,
reverses these rates in drain, and uses 50\%/50\% at both boundaries.
Independent enables permit simultaneous requests; \texttt{solve\_order}
prevents pyvsc implication bias. The phases reach near-full/near-empty and
produce bursts. At 128 cycles (seed 4701), observed write/read rates were
48.44\%/44.53\% (uniform) versus 53.91\%/50.00\% (tuned); occupancy 0--2
samples increased from 12 to 37. At 2048 tuned cycles the rates were
51.07\%/51.07\%, with longest write-only and read-only bursts of eight.
